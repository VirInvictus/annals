## 2. Parser repair (fixtures first, per the repo's own contract)

New fixtures in `tests/test_parse.py`:
- Flip `test_h3_version_heading_is_an_entry`: `###` version-led lines become subtitles, never entries. This amends spec.md deliberately.
- Phantom case: `## v0.41.1` followed by `### The 0.41.0 CPU-spin regression...` yields ONE entry whose headline is the `###` text.
- Bold lead headline (Hearthfall shape): paragraph starting with a multi-line `**bold run**` followed by plain prose; headline is the run's inner text.
- Bold whole-paragraph headline (Hearth shape).
- Plain wrapped prose paragraph headline (saforums / Colophon / lattice shape), soft-wraps joined.
- Stays bare: heading followed directly by bullets (bindery v0.23.1) yields headline "".
- Fence immunity: a ```python block containing `# v0.7.0-alpha ...` comment lines yields no entries (the meoweconomy phantom shape).
- Precedence: when both `###` and prose follow, `###` wins.

`parse.py` changes:
- Track ``` fences in the main loop and the headline scan; no entry matching inside a fence (`~~~` never appears in the corpus and stays unhandled).
- Entry matching restricted to heading levels 1-2.
- `_subtitle_after` becomes `_headline_after`: stop at the next h1/h2 entry heading; first `###` line wins; otherwise take the first paragraph (skipping bullets, table rows, headings, fences); if it starts with `**`, take the first bold run's inner text; else take the joined paragraph. Backticks and em-dashes stay verbatim per spec.
